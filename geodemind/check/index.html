Check GeodeMind records

Someone who uses GeodeMind sent you their records, or a single receipt. This page checks them here, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the page carries a lock (a Content Security Policy) that stops it from connecting to any address, and your browser's Network tab shows that nothing leaves.

        We invite you to double-check our work

        What this page can and cannot tell you is printed with every result. A record shows how many values from the sender's list were replaced before a message left, and fingerprints what left; it cannot show that a message was safe, and it says nothing about anything that was not on their list. Found something it missed, or something awkward in how this reads or works? Write to geodemind@geodeforge.com.
